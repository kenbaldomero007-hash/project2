About Us

About Us Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. Established in 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College is a Christ-centered educational institution dedicated to academic excellence, faith formation, and holistic student development. Guided by Christian values, KCC empowers learners with knowledge, character, and competence to become responsible individuals and future leaders who contribute to positive societal change.

Empowering Students Through Quality Catholic Education

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. is committed to providing meaningful educational experiences that nurture students' intellectual, spiritual, and personal growth. Through quality instruction, modern learning approaches, and dedicated educators, the institution prepares students to become competent, responsible, and values-oriented individuals.

  • Quality Education – Providing students with knowledge and skills that prepare them for their future careers.
  • Holistic Development – Developing students academically, spiritually, socially, and morally.
  • Technology-Driven Learning – Encouraging the use of modern technology and innovative learning methods to enhance educational experiences.
